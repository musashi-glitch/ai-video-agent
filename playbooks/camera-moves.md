# Camera moves (Kling)

## Principles
- **Smooth, gimbal-stable, calm.** Glide past subjects at a comfortable distance. Never fly into faces. No quick turns (motion sickness).
- **Describe an explicit route.** Vague "smooth glide" gives only a push-in. Name what passes which edge of frame and where the move ends.
- **One move per shot.**
- Avoid words: fast, swoop, banking, FPV, whip.

## Fly-through template
```
Smooth gimbal-stabilised drone shot, slow steady speed. The camera travels forward to the <side>, glides past <subject group> so they slide by at the <side> edge of frame at a comfortable distance, then makes one gentle gradual curve <direction>, drifting low alongside <destination subjects>. <Background portrait/photo> is a flat printed photograph, completely frozen.
```
Negative add-on:
`sharp turn, quick turn, whip pan, sudden direction change, spinning, camera shake, heavy motion blur, flying into faces, close-up of faces, static camera, drone visible in frame`
Plus portrait/background-face locks from `skills/motion-kling`.

## Other moves that work
| Move | Phrase |
|---|---|
| Push in | "slow gentle push in toward <subject>" |
| Locked-off | "locked-off camera, no camera movement" |
| Crane up | "slow crane up revealing <wide>" |
| Orbit | "slow quarter orbit around <subject> to the <side>" |
| Overhead | "top-down overhead, slow rotation" |

## Fixing a take
- Ends with a weird turn → cut before it (`skills/timeline-placement`), or regenerate with an explicit end of route.
- Too fast → `playbackRate` ≥ 0.85, or regenerate a longer duration with the same route.
- Goes into a face → name the distance ("at a comfortable distance", "passes beside, not toward") and add `flying into faces, close-up of faces`.
